# Privacy Policy for Phoenix Parties Karaoke

**Last Updated: November 12, 2026**

Phoenix Parties Karaoke ("the App") is a standalone desktop application. 

**1. Data Collection**
We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not use any analytics, telemetry, or tracking software.

**2. Local Files**
The App reads local media files (video, audio, and CD+G) from your computer's hard drive to play them. These files are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere. All playlist and library data is stored locally on your own machine.

**3. Third-Party Services**
The App uses the LibVLC media engine for video and audio playback. This operates entirely offline on your device and does not transmit data to VideoLAN. 

**4. Contact**
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
